# GuideConnect India

GuideConnect India is a comprehensive web platform designed to connect global travelers with the top 1% of verified local guides, scholars, historians, and food curators across 28 Indian states[cite: 1]. The platform emphasizes authentic experiences, robust traveler safety, and guaranteed escrow protection[cite: 1].

---

## Core Features & Modules

### The Tourist Experience
* **Location-First Omni Search:** Users can search by heritage city (e.g., Varanasi, Jaipur, Agra, Kerala, Ladakh), filter by experience type, set travel dates, and use live GPS location detection[cite: 1].
* **AI Matchmaker Engine:** A 4-step wizard that pairs travelers with the perfect guide by evaluating destination preferences, travel style (e.g., Spiritual, Culinary), guide personality, and group dynamics (solo, family, couple)[cite: 1].
* **Interactive Roster Map:** A live, interactive map powered by Leaflet.js that allows users to zoom into regions and view available local guides via golden pins[cite: 1].
* **Curated Heritage Journeys:** Categories encompass Spiritual Ghats & Temples, Heritage & Palaces, Safe Spice Trails & Food, and Bazaars & Artisans[cite: 1].
* **VIP Royal Lounge:** Exclusive luxury offerings, including private after-hours access to Amber Palace, Taj Mahal helicopter transfers from Delhi NCR, and 3-day private Indology scholar escorts[cite: 1].
* **Live In-App Chat:** A messaging system featuring auto-translate capabilities and quick-reply suggestions to communicate effortlessly with local guides[cite: 1].

### Local Guide Partner Portal
* **Dual-Persona System:** Users can easily toggle between the "Tourist" view and the "Guide Portal" interface[cite: 1].
* **Performance Analytics:** Guides have access to a dashboard tracking total platform earnings, completed tours, traveler ratings, and verification status[cite: 1].
* **Booking Management:** A streamlined interface to review incoming booking requests, manage monthly revenue trends, and update hourly rates or availability statuses (Available, Busy, Offline)[cite: 1].
* **Guide Onboarding Wizard:** A dedicated 3-step application wizard for new guides to submit their details, state their hourly rates, and upload their Ministry of Tourism license for vetting[cite: 1].

### Trust, Safety & Payments
* **100% Escrow Checkout:** Payments are securely held in escrow and released to the guide only after the tour is completed[cite: 1]. Supported methods include Credit Card, UPI/QR, NetBanking, and 1-Click Global Pay (Apple Pay, Google Pay)[cite: 1].
* **24/7 Tourist SOS Hub:** A dedicated emergency modal providing immediate one-tap dialing to Police Dispatch (112), Medical/Ambulance (108), and the Ministry of Tourism multilingual infoline (1363)[cite: 1].
* **Live GPS Safety Beacon:** Allows travelers to broadcast their real-time coordinates and their guide's verified details directly to family WhatsApp contacts and local Tourist Police[cite: 1].
* **Strict Verification Tiers:** All guides undergo background checks and are categorized by tiers, including Tier 4 (MoT / ASI Licensed) and Tier 2+ (Police Cleared)[cite: 1].

## How It Works

1. **Search Destination:** Enter a city to instantly surface local guides with verified government credentials[cite: 1].
2. **AI Smart Matching:** The algorithm evaluates language, interests, walking pace, and budget to rank ideal matches[cite: 1].
3. **Escrow Protected Booking:** Book securely with digital escrow[cite: 1].
4. **SafeWalk & Review:** Enjoy the exploration with 24/7 GPS monitoring and emergency helpline access[cite: 1].
